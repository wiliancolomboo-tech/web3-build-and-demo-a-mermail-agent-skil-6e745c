1/3 🚨 Introducing the Mermail Agent Skill for @solana: Autonomous AI agents can swap tokens and manage treasuries, but they've been cut off from real-world communication.

Until now. 

We built the production cryptographic bridge between Solana agents & enterprise email via @resend. 🧵👇

2/3 🛠️ How it works:
1️⃣ Agent triggers an alert or invoice.
2️⃣ Mermail anchor program hashes payload & locks anti-spam fee in a MailReceipt PDA.
3️⃣ Enterprise relay pushes a DKIM-signed email with on-chain cryptographic proofs & Solscan verification links.

Zero spam. 100% provenance.
